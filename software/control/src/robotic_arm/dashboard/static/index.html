Limits

Joint degrees. The arm's own limits for are ; these narrow them for this session.

Motor-shaft degrees around the encoder's zero, and motor RPM (ceiling ).

Sign

+1 if the encoder counts up as the joint turns positive. config.JOINTS has , which isn't confirmed on the arm yet. Use the direction check (Bring-up) to measure it. Changing the sign here clears the reference.

Reference

The joint angle at the encoder's zero. Homing sets it (the switch's edge is home_deg °). You can also tell the page where the joint is now. It's lost when the board loses power.

Home switch

IN_1: —

The KY-003 pulls IN_1 low when it sees its magnet (the board pulls the pin up). Turn the joint through the switch by hand or by jog and watch this change before you home it.

Homing (0x90, then 0x91)

Homing turns toward − (every joint's switch is at its lower end) until the switch turns on. Which motor direction that is depends on the joint's belt or gear and on its sign: watch the first run and press STOP if it heads the wrong way.

Moves back and forth between A and B, and reads the error at each end after a dwell. It stops on a stall.

A single step from where the joint is. The encoder is polled every 5 ms, and the page reports rise time, overshoot, settling time and final error.

Moves end to end at each speed. Over the middle half of each move it records the measured speed, the current (0x38, V1.0.9 only) and the angle error.

Which CAN ids answer

Every board ships at id 1, so two new boards on one bus answer as one. Connect one at a time while you set the ids.

Checks

Firmware

Reads 0x40, checks whether the board answers V1.0.9's newer reads, and reads its work mode and settings with 0x00.

Direction (the sign)

Steps the encoder up by a small amount, then asks which way the joint (or the shaft, in bench mode) turned, and steps back. This works before the reference is set.

Home switch zone

Moves slowly from A to B and back while polling IN_1 every 10 ms, and reports where the switch turns on and off in each direction.

Findings

What the checks measured, to copy into config.py or open_issues.md. The page doesn't edit files.
